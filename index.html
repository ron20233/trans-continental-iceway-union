Four goals, one iceway

Pave, map, farm and maintain.

Pave

We pave iceways throughout the Nether with the objective of connecting the nations of EarthMC.

Map

A reliable, mapped system for fast travel between nations, showing every stop, intersection, transfer and line. We also explore and map every non-TCI iceway and railroad in the Nether and the Overworld.

Farm

We farm blue ice in the Arctic and run automatic ice farms, selling packed and blue ice to players, towns and nations in exchange for gold. New customers get good deals, returning customers get better ones. Can you build efficient automatic ice farms? Open a ticket on Discord, we pay per farm built.

Maintain

Most highways are already paved, so our current mission is upkeep. All TCI lines are constantly renovated and repaired, and some stations are being transformed through beautification projects.

Bill of Rights

All members of the TCIU are instructed to always follow these bylaws.

Unintentional griefs are common when constructing iceways, so assume the best before you assume the worst. However, if you believe a TCIU member was responsible for a grief that relates to a TCI iceway, contact EarthMC staff.

The actions of our members directly reflect on how EarthMC players view the TCIU. We are by no means telling our members to be pacifists, but treating any other player as complete garbage for no good reason will never be tolerated. Members only have to abide by this bylaw while representing the TCIU in some way, or while chatting in the TCIU Discord server. To report server rule-breaking activity, contact EarthMC staff.

We will not, and will never, build a stop to a town whose sole purpose is running a casino. The TCI still serves towns with casinos, as there are still players with homes who reside in those towns. If the only major goal of your town is to run a casino, the TCIU will not be working with your settlement.

Do not do anything that will cause unnecessary conflict. Put simply: don't do what you're doing if you know it's stupid. If you believe something may cause an unneeded problem, you can think outside the box, ask someone who knows a solution, or not do it at all.

Our members should always care for others, themselves, and the job given to them. Members should not feel like they have to, which leads to job neglect, but rather want to complete their tasks. Be aware of other people's behavior, monitor your own health, and take action when needed. Get off Minecraft, take a break, go touch grass, or even take a shower.
